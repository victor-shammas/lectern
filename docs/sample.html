03

Pause on the number before saying anything. Let it land.

Context matters here, so walk through where it came from. Roughly 60% of downloads came from a single post on a Mac-focused forum in week one, which I didn't plan; someone from the beta shared it. The remaining 40% was a long tail: App Store search (mostly for "rename files"), two newsletter mentions, and word of mouth.

If asked about revenue: one-time price of $14.99, so around $13k after Apple's cut under the Small Business Program. Don't round up.

If asked about refunds: 11 so far, mostly people expecting it to rename files in iCloud Drive without granting access first. That's a UX fix on the roadmap.

Likely question: "Would you do subscriptions?" Answer: not for a utility this size. People are visibly relieved when they see a one-time price, and it shows in the reviews.
